03What the app can and can't do

The app estimates distance from Bluetooth signal strength. That estimate is about ±30 % at best: walls, bodies, metal, water and other devices weaken and reflect the signal, so a device can read closer or farther than it is. The map's likely spot is a probability, not a position. In particular:

  • A device can be found only while it is switched on and advertising. Many devices advertise only in certain states (earbuds out of the case, for example) or not at all while connected to a phone.
  • Devices such as phones, AirPods and AirTags change their Bluetooth identity from time to time, so the app may not recognize them as the same device later.
  • Ring works only on devices that support the standard Bluetooth Immediate Alert service.

We can't guarantee that the app will find any particular device, and the app is not a safety, security or emergency tool. Don't rely on it to locate people, pets or anything where a missed or wrong reading could cause harm.
